Faceless videos, made in your browser. Your files never leave this device.
1 Script

Each paragraph (blank line between) becomes one scene. Put *stars* around spoken words the hand should also write on the board. Use [square brackets] for board-only text that isn't read out or captioned. Curly brackets like {idea} pick a doodle.

2 Visuals

A hand draws each scene on a board. Doodles are picked automatically from your words, or choose one by typing its name in curly brackets, like {money}. Uploaded images are turned into sketches: image 1 is drawn in scene 1, image 2 in scene 2, and so on.

Doodles (tap to add to the script)

Clips and images play one per scene, in order, and repeat if you have fewer than scenes. With none, an animated background is used.

    3 Voice & music

    Upload a voiceover or record one with your mic. The video length matches the voiceover; silence at the start and end is detected automatically.

    No voiceover: captions are timed at a natural reading pace.
