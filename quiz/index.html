    The full guide (2026)

    Tax-Free Savings Account

    CELI (TFSA)

    Launched in 2009. Interest, dividends and investment gains inside it are never taxed, not even when you take the money out.

    Who can open one
    18+, Canadian resident, valid SIN
    2026 limit
    $7,000
    Total room (18+ in 2009, never contributed)
    $109,000
    Unused room
    Carries forward with no limit
    Withdrawals
    Any time, tax-free. The amount comes back as room on January 1 of the next year.
    Tax deduction?
    No
    Over-contribution
    1% tax per month on the extra
    What you can hold
    Cash, GICs, bonds, stocks, ETFs
    The law
    Income Tax Act, s. 146.2
    First Home Savings Account

    CELIAPP (FHSA)

    The best of an RRSP and a CELI: contributions are tax-deductible, and withdrawals to buy your first home are tax-free.

    Who can open one
    18+, Canadian resident, first-time buyer
    First-time buyer means
    You didn't live in a home you owned this year or in the previous 4 calendar years
    Yearly limit
    $8,000
    Lifetime limit
    $40,000
    Unused room
    Carries forward, but only up to $8,000. Room starts the year you open the account, so open it early, even with $0.
    How long it stays open
    Up to 15 years
    If you don't buy
    Move it to your RRSP tax-free (no RRSP room needed)
    Over-contribution
    1% tax per month on the extra
    The law
    Income Tax Act, s. 146.6
    Registered Retirement Savings Plan

    RRSP (REER)

    Contributions are deducted from your income, so you pay less tax now. The money grows tax-sheltered and is taxed when you withdraw it, ideally in retirement when your income is lower.

    2026 limit
    18% of your 2025 earned income, up to $33,810
    Your exact room
    On your Notice of Assessment or CRA My Account
    Unused room
    Carries forward with no limit
    Withdrawals
    Taxed as income, with tax withheld at the source. The room is lost.
    Home Buyers' Plan
    Borrow up to $60,000 tax-free for a first home, then pay it back over 15 years
    Deadline
    60 days after year-end for the previous tax year
    Must close by
    End of the year you turn 71 (convert it to a RRIF or annuity)
    Over-contribution
    1% tax per month on anything over a $2,000 buffer
